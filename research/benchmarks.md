# One verb, stacked passes, weekly codes win

The four benchmarks follow one template. A single hold-and-release or walk-on action pays a currency on the first try, an exponential equipment or rebirth ladder turns that currency into bigger numbers, and depth, distance or rarity sits physically "further out". Money comes mostly from cheap automation passes and stacked multiplier or luck passes, and a numbered update with a code ships every one to three weeks. Two identities are solid. **Chicken Farm 🐣 by Skandi Studios** (created 23 May 2026) reported a **123,756 peak CCU** and about **113M visits** by September. **Deep Fishing by LazyGames.** (created 18 Jul 2026) reported about **17.6M visits and an 18.9K peak CCU**. The other two are uncertain. "Hit a Golf Ball" is most likely Tiny Loop's game, with Golf Training! as the alternative, and "Collect Planets" is most likely RockyStar Studios' game, with Mine a Planet as the alternative. Neither has reliable scale data, and RockyStar's game looks like a ~500-CCU mid-size title rather than a viral hit. Dig for Dragon Eggs already matches the template on its first minute, its number curve and its odds disclosure. The benchmarks point to five changes for us to consider:

- a steeper, longer rebirth ladder;
- a cheap 29–79 R$ entry pass and tiered luck passes in place of the single Lucky Hatcher;
- earlier offline income;
- a mutation/Golden layer and silhouette Dragondex at soft launch;
- codes and a weekly numbered update cadence from day one, which is when Roblox's June 2026 discovery changes start scoring 28-day retention and co-play.

> **Read this before relying on any number.** Every researcher was blocked from fetching pages directly (roblox.com, Rolimons, RoMonitor, DevForum, fan wikis and press sites all returned egress errors). **Every finding below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries, not full page reads.** Many sources are unofficial SEO fan wikis that contradict each other. Most metrics are undated snapshots, and several conflict (Chicken Farm visits appear as 16.8M, 56.2M and 113.3M). **The identities of "Hit a Golf Ball" and "Collect Planets" are uncertain** and need confirming by the user. No source gives timed first-session milestones for any of the four games, so every pacing comparison is inferred from loop descriptions. Confidence is marked throughout, and a hand-verification checklist is near the end. All design lessons are **recommendations, not decisions**.

## Every loop pays on the first press and puts rarity farther away

The core loops are almost interchangeable, and that is the main lesson. Hit a Golf Ball: "hold your Left Mouse Button to power up your swing and release it to hit the ball", steer with A/D to hit boosters mid-flight, and distance pays Coins ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide)). Deep Fishing: "throw the lure as far as your rod allows, pull several fish out of the water on a single cast, sell the haul, and buy something that lets you throw farther" ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/roblox-deep-fishing-guide-best-rods-and-upgrade-order/)). Chicken Farm starts every player with **$1, enough for the first chicken**, and runs on walk-on "Buy 1/5/25/100" pads that behave identically on mobile and PC ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/chicken-farm-a-beginner-s-guide)). In all three, the first reward arrives within seconds and the first purchase follows immediately. Deep Fishing adds a burst of several fish per cast, so every action is a multi-reward moment. Chicken Farm adds a **30-second fluctuating Egg Multiplier (0.5x–1.5x)** that turns an idle loop into a small "when do I sell?" decision ([TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/chicken-farm-beginner-guide/)).

Progression differs in emphasis, as the table shows.

Sources for the table: Deep Fishing rods and stats ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/all-rods-deep-fishing); [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/roblox-deep-fishing-guide-best-rods-and-upgrade-order/)); Deep Fishing islands, rarity and badges ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/deep-fishing-every-fish-you-can-catch-by-island/); [deepfishing.wiki](https://deepfishing.wiki/)); Chicken Farm rebirths ([AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/chicken-farm-rebirth-requirements-and-rewards-explained/); [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/chicken-farm-rebirth-guide)); Gold Chickens ([earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/chicken-farm-codes)); Golf rebirth and worlds ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide)); Collect Planets systems ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/hiring-thumbnail-artist-for-collect-planets-%E2%80%94-20day-%E2%80%94-ongoing/4894609)).

Three structural patterns recur across the table. The first is the **exponential cost, roughly linear power** ladder: Deep Fishing's rod luck and strength rise about linearly while price multiplies ×2–×3.5 per tier. That gives players a purchase target every few minutes for the whole life of the game. The second is **rarity as geography**: "rare fish … are a geography problem before they are a luck problem" ([deepfishing.wiki](https://deepfishing.wiki/guides/how-to-catch-rare-fish/)), which is exactly our "deeper layer, better egg" rule. The third is a **second collection axis on top of rarity**, in the form of mutations, Gold variants or Rainbow tiers, which give duplicates value. Prestige design varies most. Chicken Farm's rebirth multipliers explode geometrically, and the studio adds roughly one new rebirth per update, so live ops lengthens the prestige ladder instead of building new systems. Hit a Golf Ball keeps gear through rebirth to soften the reset. Deep Fishing appears to have no rebirth at all and relies on its rod ladder and the 101-fish index. Notably, **none of the four uses real-time hatch timers**. Chicken Farm's timer is egg-laying, and its chickens are bought instantly. That makes our timed nests a differentiator, and also an untested one.

First-session timing is the biggest evidence gap. No source gives times to first upgrade, first rare or first rebirth for any of the four. The one pacing signal that stands out is that Chicken Farm's codes hand out **43M–407M cash**, far above the **5M** Rebirth 1 cost ([games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/chicken-farm-codes-for-september-2026/)). A code-redeeming newcomer can therefore rebirth almost immediately, which means codes double as an onboarding accelerator.

## Cheap automation and stacked luck passes carry the store

The verified price data is thin but consistent. Deep Fishing sells nine gamepasses from **99 to 599 R$**: Lucky 99, Super Lucky 249, Ultra Lucky 599, Double Coins 399, More Mutations 349, Fish Magnet 99, Auto Sell 149, Double XP 249 and VIP 249, about 2,441 R$ for the full set ([deep-fishing.wiki](https://deep-fishing.wiki/tier-list/deep-fishing-gamepasses-tier-list)). Chicken Farm's reported passes are strikingly cheap: **Auto Collect Money 29 R$, Auto Collect Eggs 79 R$ and Double Eggs 499 R$**, 607 R$ in total ([Nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/chicken-farm-codes/)). Its consumable boosts are x3 Eggs for 5 minutes and x2/x5 Cash for 5–10 minutes, and its Lucky Blocks cost cash and grow pricier with each opening ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/chicken-farm-codes/); [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/roblox-chicken-farm-codes/)). Hit a Golf Ball reportedly runs about fifteen passes: **twelve tiered Power Multiplier passes**, x2 Money, and three premium companions named **Forest Dragon, Gold Dragon and Kraken**. Prices were not found ([hit-a-golf-ball.wiki](https://hit-a-golf-ball.wiki/guides/)). For Collect Planets no store data surfaced, only the unverified ~150K R$/day claim. If that claim were true it would mean about 300 R$ per CCU per day, an unusually high figure. Older simulator references put 2x Coins at 149–799 R$, auto-clickers at 50–399 R$ and VIP at 349 R$ ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/18225070); [Saber Simulator wiki](https://roblox-saber-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses)). No developer-product price list was retrievable for any benchmark, because every store tab was blocked.

Read together, the pattern is a **low-friction entry pass that sells off a chore** (29–149 R$), a **mid-tier multiplier** (249–499 R$), and a **ladder of the same effect at rising prices** (99/249/599 luck, or twelve Power passes), which anchors the top tier. Benchmarks for payers sit in a hard frame. GameAnalytics' 2026 Roblox data puts **median ARPPU at $0.70, the top 5% at $4.80 and the top 1% at $55.31** ([GameDevReports/GameAnalytics](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox)). Payer-conversion "norms" of 2–3% for simulators come only from secondary blogs and should be treated as low confidence ([ZehnStudio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/glossary/conversion-rate/)). Roblox also said its Q2 2026 bookings shortfall came from lower per-hour monetization "most notably with younger cohorts in the U.S. and Canada" ([Roblox Q2 2026 letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Roblox-Q2-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)). For a kids' game that argues for cheap, clear purchases over expensive ones.

Two platform rules constrain the catalogue. **Paid random items** (Robux eggs, crates, lucky blocks) must show numeric odds that sum to 100% before purchase and must check `PolicyService:ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` ([Creator Hub](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)). **Rewarded video ads**, open to all ads-eligible creators since 18 Feb 2026, may be shown only to users aged 13+ and cannot reward randomized items ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rewarded-video-ads-are-now-available-to-all-ads-eligible-creators/4063278)). Most of our audience is under 13, so ads are a minor revenue line at best.

## Weekly code-tagged updates and 28-day retention drive discovery

Live ops is where the confirmed hits separate from the rest. LazyGames shipped **8+ numbered updates in about ten weeks**, each with a code named after its content ("Frostmere", "Mutations", "kraken", "update8"), redeemed through the Store menu ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/deep-fishing-codes-roblox/)). Chicken Farm shipped **Update 10 by 5 Sep 2026**, about 15 weeks after creation, and pairs each update with an **"Admin Abuse + Update" event roughly every 21 days, always on a Saturday** ([AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/chicken-farm-admin-abuse-update-schedule/)). Its codes mark community milestones ("50MVisits", "THANKSFOR100K", "666klikes", "700klikes"), and limited codes appear first on Discord ([games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/chicken-farm-codes-for-september-2026/)). Golf Training! carried an "[UPD4]" tag in its title ([Medal.tv](https://medal.tv/games/roblox/experiences/1b4bIlv_blA6jN/upd4golf-training)). Bracketed update tags and emoji prefixes are common across the wave as a freshness signal. By contrast, Tiny Loop's game had **no verified codes as of September 2026** ([urgametips](https://urgametips.com/hit-a-golf-ball-codes/)), and Collect Planets has no codes coverage at all. The smaller footprint goes together with weaker codes activity, though the direction of cause is unclear. RockyStar pays a thumbnail artist **$20/day on an ongoing basis** ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/hiring-thumbnail-artist-for-collect-planets-%E2%80%94-20day-%E2%80%94-ongoing/4894609)), which suggests it treats discovery as a continuous thumbnail-testing job.

Roblox's discovery rules changed under all of these games. Since the **15 Jun 2026** "Optimizing Discovery" rollout, Recommended For You scores over a **28-day window** with separate D1, D2–7 and D8–28 retention signals. It splits qPTR into play-through, session-quality and spend signals, and adds **7-day intentional co-play days**, which third-party coverage calls the headline signal ([Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/why-roblox-s-new-discovery-algorithm-favors-games-that-bring-friends-together)). Roblox said the old system "could disproportionately favor games that win attention with exciting thumbnails but don't deliver long-term value" ([Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-optimizing-discovery-how-great-games-reach-millions-of-players-on-roblox)). One creator reported revenue down as much as 70% in week one, then a portfolio "performing better than ever" three weeks later ([X/EvanZir](https://x.com/EvanZir/article/2074281929575174603)). For retention, the 2026 Roblox median is **D1 10.3%, D7 1.6%, D30 0.5%**, and the top 1% reach **D1 22.2%, D7 9.1%, D30 4.7%** ([GameDevReports/GameAnalytics](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox)). Facial age checks gate chat globally since January 2026, and users chat only within age brackets ([Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat)). That makes **co-play mechanics that work without chat**, such as shared server boosts, friend boosts and server-wide events, more valuable for a kids' game. Collect Planets' friend boost and our purchasable Server Egg Shower both fit this.

Why did these games succeed? The sources offer no developer interviews or analytics, so the answer is synthesis rather than testimony. Pocket Tactics calls Deep Fishing satisfying precisely because "there's nothing deep about this game" ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/deep-fishing-codes)). Chicken Farm is described as an idle tycoon that "hooks you fast", with offline egg generation and YouTube/TikTok spread ([games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/chicken-farm-codes-for-september-2026/)). The consistent recipe is an instantly readable verb-first title, a first reward within seconds, visible "farther equals rarer" progress, cheap automation, and an update with a code every one to three weeks. The format is easy to copy: Chicken Farm templates are already sold to developers ([BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/chicken-farm-full-game-template.127310/)). So a novel twist matters more than the base loop, and for us that twist is timed dragon hatching.

## What Dig for Dragon Eggs should keep, change and add

Our current design (`/root/Documents/apps/dragon-eggs/DESIGN.md`, sections 0, 2, 3.3, 3.4, 6 and 7) already lines up with the benchmarks on the first minute. It targets a first egg under 20 s, a first hatch under 45 s and a first upgrade under 90 s. Hold-to-dig is the same hold-and-release verb as golf and fishing. The pickaxe ladder (200 gold to 5.6B, about 7.4 orders of magnitude) matches Deep Fishing's rod span almost exactly. Rebirth keeps the collection, which echoes Hit a Golf Ball keeping clubs. Odds are always shown, and Robux eggs are excluded from MVP. Where we diverge, the divergence is mostly in the long tail and the store. Every row below is a **recommendation, not a decision**.

Two retention targets follow from the GameAnalytics distribution: **D1 at or above 15–20% and D7 at or above 5%**. These sit between the median and the top 1%, and are our inference rather than a published bar. They belong in the M1/M2 exit criteria next to the existing 70%-reach-Stone-Pick funnel goal. One caution applies to the Chicken Farm lesson. Its codes worth nearly 100x the R1 cost show that generous codes speed up onboarding, but if our codes give gold, they should be capped relative to the current layer. Otherwise they let new players skip the seal-and-craft beat that our playtest questions are designed to evaluate.

## Conclusion

The benchmarks say less about *what* to build than about *how long it has to keep giving*. Dig for Dragon Eggs' first five minutes already match or beat the speed of the hits. The real gaps are at minute 60 and on day 8: a rebirth ladder that tops out early with gentle linear multipliers, offline income deferred to later, a duplicate-value layer parked in M3, and codes and updates treated as post-launch polish. Since June 2026 Roblox's recommender measures exactly that 28-day tail and co-play, so these choices now decide discovery as well as retention. The timed hatch is our one structural differentiator in a genre where the base loop is sold as a template, so it should be protected and made the centre of the return hook. That means offline income plus ready eggs on return, with timers kept short early.

On monetization, the hits point to many small, clear purchases rather than a few large ones: a sub-80 R$ chore-removal pass, tiered luck passes, and deterministic premium dragons, which suit a young audience whose per-hour spend Roblox reports is falling. The highest-value next step costs almost nothing. Someone opens the four store tabs and plays each game for 20 minutes with a stopwatch, which turns this snippet-based picture into the verified benchmark set the price and pacing calls need.
