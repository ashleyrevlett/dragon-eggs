## Core loop: action, output, feel, controls, active vs idle

### Takeaway
The loop is buy chickens, have them lay eggs on a timer, walk the eggs to the Farmer's Stall deposit pad, and get cash sold at one egg per second (upgradeable). Merging duplicates gives a higher tier. The game mixes active hauling with idle production. Eggs are laid offline. Servers hold 6 players.

### Cited Findings
- "You buy chickens, collect the eggs they lay, sell those eggs for cash, and pour the money back into more chickens." — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/roblox-chicken-farm-beginner-guide-eggs-merging-and-rebirth/)
- Players start with $1, enough for the first chicken. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/chicken-farm-a-beginner-s-guide); [chickenfarmgame.wiki](https://chickenfarmgame.wiki/)
- Buying works by walking onto squares marked "Buy 1", "Buy 5", "Buy 25" or "Buy 100". Movement-based pads work identically on mobile and PC. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/chicken-farm-a-beginner-s-guide)
- Selling: "stand on the deposit button to hand over all your eggs at once. The Farmer then sells one egg per second and pays you cash for each sale. You can upgrade that sell rate later." — [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/chicken-farm-beginner-guide/)
- The Egg Shop processes deposited eggs into cash, and Process Level upgrades improve throughput. — [search summary of beginner guides](https://chickenfarmgame.wiki/)
- Egg Multiplier: unlocks after a few merges and "changes every 30 seconds and ranges from 0.5x to 1.5x". Depositing above 1x gives extra eggs; below 1x gives fewer. — [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/chicken-farm-beginner-guide/)
- One tip: collect at least 20 eggs before selling, and deposit while the multiplier is above 1x. — [chickenfarmgame.wiki](https://chickenfarmgame.wiki/)
- "Your chickens keep laying eggs even while you're away." — [Rolimons / game description](https://www.rolimons.com/game/137233438285284)
- "Servers hold only six players, so the experience stays calm and personal rather than crowded." — [RoboLibrary](https://robolibrary.org/wikis/chicken-farm)

### Inferences
- The 30-second fluctuating Egg Multiplier is a light "timing skill" layer on an idle loop. It rewards checking in and choosing when to sell, which is a cheap, kid-readable decision. The dragon game could do the same with something like a fluctuating "market price" for selling dragons or egg shards.
- The one-egg-per-second sell queue creates a visible stream of payouts, a steady stream of feedback, and gives an obvious upgrade target in sell speed.

### Gaps
- No precise per-chicken lay intervals or egg values were found.
- No info on juice or feedback (sounds, popups) and no mobile UI specifics were found. YouTube was not watched.

## Eggs and animals: timers, rarities, odds, selling vs keeping, variants

### Takeaway
Chickens are the producers and eggs are the currency feedstock. Rarity comes mainly from **deterministic merging** (duplicates make the next tier), with RNG layered on through **Lucky Blocks**. There are 36+ chicken tiers, Gold Chickens came in Update 10, and some high tiers drop special eggs or Lucky Blocks.

### Cited Findings
- "Stand on the Merge pad to fuse up to three matching chickens into one higher-tier chicken. Merging frees nest space and increases income per slot." — [AllThings.How / guides (search summary)](https://allthings.how/roblox-chicken-farm-beginner-guide-eggs-merging-and-rebirth/). This conflicts with Rolimons / games.gg wording, which says "merging two chickens of the same type produces a rarer breed." — [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/chicken-farm-codes-for-september-2026/)
- There are 36+ chicken types. The tiers include Basic, Duck, Fried, Snowman, Frozen and Angel. Angel, Crystal and Royal are among the most valuable. Frozen is tier 15 and drops ice eggs and Lucky Blocks. — [chickenfarmwiki.wiki](http://chickenfarmwiki.wiki/chickens); [chicken-farm.wiki](https://chicken-farm.wiki/)
- Basic Lucky Block odds: Bee (Tier 4) 75%, Mushroom (Tier 5) 20%, Dummy (Tier 6) 5%. Odds for the other blocks are not published. — [search summary of code guides, e.g. Dexerto / Insider Gaming](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/chicken-farm-codes-3395955/)
- Lucky Blocks "cost Cash to unbox, cannot be stored in your backpack… must either be opened instantly or held until you have enough Cash. Each opening increases the price of the next block." Named types include Basic, Gummy and Hacker. — [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/roblox-chicken-farm-codes/); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/chicken-farm-codes/)
- Gold Chickens were added in Update 10 on 5 Sep 2026. — [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/chicken-farm-codes)
- Early on, a full nest of basic birds out-earns one rare bird, so filling slots comes first. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/chicken-farm-a-beginner-s-guide)

### Inferences
- Chickens are not sold. Eggs are sold and chickens are consumed by merging. This matters for the dragon design: "sell or keep" in Chicken Farm is effectively "merge or keep," and the sell decision sits on the produced resource.
- Gold Chickens are probably a variant or mutation layer (unconfirmed).
- No hatch timer for chickens was found. Chickens are bought instantly, so the timer is egg-laying, not hatching. Timer-based hatching would set the dragon game apart.

### Gaps
- Two-way versus three-way merge is unresolved. It may differ by version or allow both.
- Lay timers, a full rarity table, mutation mechanics and Gold Chicken odds were not found.

## Progression: upgrades, nests, rebirth, multipliers, automation, offline, numbers

### Takeaway
Progression runs through buy tiers, upgrade levels, Process Level and sell rate, then gem upgrades unlocked at Rebirth 3. Rebirth gives a permanent cash multiplier plus 2 new buy tiers each time. Reported multipliers run from x2 at Rebirth 1 to x6767 at Rebirth 8, and costs escalate from 5M to 25 Sx cash.

### Cited Findings
- Rebirth was added in Update 5. Each rebirth "wipes your current run but hands back a permanent cash multiplier and two extra buy tiers." — [AllThings.How rebirth guide](https://allthings.how/chicken-farm-rebirth-requirements-and-rewards-explained/)
- Rebirth table (reported):

  | Rebirth | Cost | Multiplier |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 5M | x2 |
  | 2 | 150B | x5 |
  | 3 | 500B | x10 |
  | 4–6 | TBC | TBC |
  | 7 | 1 Qn | x2500 |
  | 8 | 25 Sx | x6767 (the cap at writing) |

  Each rebirth adds +2 buy tiers. — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/chicken-farm-rebirth-requirements-and-rewards-explained/); [Sportskeeda rebirth guide](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/chicken-farm-rebirth-guide)
- Rebirth resets cash, eggs, chickens and processing level. — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/chicken-farm-rebirth-requirements-and-rewards-explained/)
- The rebirth button "on the right of the screen turns red" when affordable. — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/chicken-farm-rebirth-requirements-and-rewards-explained/)
- Update 7 added gem upgrades (unlock at Rebirth 3), 2 Lucky Blocks, 2 rebirths and 4 buy tiers. — [search summary of guides](https://chickenfarmgame.wiki/)
- Update 8 (live by 22 Aug 2026) added 4 chickens, 10 upgrade levels, a Lucky Block, a rebirth, 2 buy tiers and balance changes. — [chicken-farm.wiki / earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/chicken-farm)
- Update 10 (5 Sep 2026) added events, new gem upgrades, a Lucky Block, Gold Chickens, an obby, a rebirth, 2 buy tiers and balance changes. — [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/chicken-farm-codes)
- Automation is sold as gamepasses (Auto Collect Money, Auto Collect Eggs; see Monetization). Offline laying is confirmed. — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/137233438285284)
- Codes grant amounts such as 407.07M or 43.43M cash, which shows number escalation in the hundreds of millions as early handouts. — [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/chicken-farm-codes-for-september-2026/); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/chicken-farm-codes/)

### Inferences
- The rebirth cap rises with each update, about +1 rebirth per update. Live ops extends the prestige ladder instead of adding new systems. The "x6767" figure is a "67" meme number, a kid-culture wink.
- "Nests" act as the plot or slot cap, and merging frees slots. That means space pressure, not cash, drives merging.

### Gaps
- No offline earnings cap was found. No nest count or expansion costs, and no Rebirth 4–6 values, were found.

## First-session pacing

### Takeaway
Only partial data exists. The first purchase happens at second zero ($1 start), merging comes within the first minutes, the Egg Multiplier unlocks after "a few batches" of merges, and Rebirth 1 needs 5M cash. No source gives timed milestones.

## Monetization

### Takeaway
Reported gamepasses are cheap automation plus one premium multiplier: Auto Collect Money 29 R$, Auto Collect Eggs 79 R$ and Double Eggs 499 R$, 607 R$ in total. Paid Lucky Blocks and boosts are likely developer products but are unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- "Auto Collect Money (29 Robux), Auto Collect Eggs (79 Robux), and Double Eggs (499 Robux) — totaling 607 Robux." — [search summary of code guides (Nerdschalk / Insider Gaming / Pro Game Guides)](https://nerdschalk.com/chicken-farm-codes/)
- Boost types in the economy: Egg Multipliers (more eggs per chicken, e.g. x3 Eggs for 5 minutes), Cash Boosts (x2 or x5 cash for 5–10 minutes) and Lucky Blocks. — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/chicken-farm-codes/); [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/chicken-farm-codes-for-september-2026/)

### Inferences
- Pricing automation very cheaply (29 R$) is a low-friction first purchase aimed at kids. Collecting by hand is the friction being sold off.

### Gaps
- No verified developer-product list (Robux Lucky Blocks, timed boosts, server boosts), starter packs or limited offers. The store tab was not reachable. A gems premium currency exists, but whether it can be bought with Robux is unknown.

## Live ops, events and social

### Takeaway
There is a regular cadence of numbered updates: Update 5 introduced rebirth, Update 8 landed around 22 Aug and Update 10 on 5 Sep 2026. Each update is paired with an "Admin Abuse + Update" event about every 21 days, always on a Saturday. Codes are frequent and milestone-based, and the Discord gets codes first. No trading or stealing was found. Servers hold 6 players.

### Cited Findings
- Admin Abuse + Update is recurring, "roughly every ~21 days, and every run so far has landed on a Saturday." The developer hands out admin-style powers, then ships the update right after. — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/chicken-farm-admin-abuse-update-schedule/)
- Update 10 added an "Events" system and an obby. — [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/chicken-farm-codes)
- Codes are tied to milestones (50MVisits, THANKSFOR100K, 666klikes, 700klikes) and updates (update8). Limited-time codes have redemption caps and appear first on the official Discord. — [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/chicken-farm-codes-for-september-2026/); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/chicken-farm-codes/)
- The Codes button is in the top-left of the UI. — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/chicken-farm-codes/)

### Inferences
- About 10 updates in about 15 weeks (23 May to 5 Sep) works out to roughly one update every 1–2 weeks early on, slowing to about three weeks per update with the admin-abuse pairing. This is inferred from the update numbers and dates.

### Gaps
- No evidence of trading, plot visiting or stealing. Event details beyond admin abuse were not found. DevForum and X posts from the developer were not found.

## Why it succeeded

### Takeaway
No press piece or developer statement explaining the success was found. The commonly cited drivers are: a very simple loop that hooks fast, merging, offline production, cheap automation, a frequent update and admin-abuse cadence, and generous milestone codes, all amplified by YouTube and TikTok.

### Cited Findings
- It is one of the idle tycoons that "hooks you fast," and "offline egg generation means your farm keeps working even when you log off." Spread came through YouTube and TikTok creators. — [games.gg / search summary](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/chicken-farm-codes-for-september-2026/); [TikTok discover page](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/farming-and-friends-roblox-chicken-farm); [YouTube "Roblox Chicken Farm is INSANE"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJcoc02bumg)
- About 99% like ratio. — [RoboLibrary](https://robolibrary.org/wikis/chicken-farm)

### Inferences
- The game fits the 2025–26 Roblox "simple idle plus offline progress" wave, in the lineage of Grow a Garden. Small 6-player servers likely aid performance on low-end mobile devices (inferred).

### Gaps
- No analytics-firm or press analysis, no creator interview and no Reddit discussion were retrieved.
