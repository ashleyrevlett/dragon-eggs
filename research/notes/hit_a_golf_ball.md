## 3. Core loop, feel, and controls

### Takeaway
The game has one action: **hold to charge a swing, release to launch the ball**. Distance becomes Coins, and Coins buy power upgrades, clubs and balls. While the ball flies you can steer it left and right to hit obstacles and boosters for bonuses.

### Cited Findings
- "Hold your Left Mouse Button to power up your swing and release it to hit the ball." A and D (left/right) "try and hit as many objects along the way as possible" — [Sportskeeda beginner's guide](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide) (via search summary)
- "The further the ball lands, the more Coins you earn, providing you with the funds needed to purchase club and ball upgrades" — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide)
- "Each map … features an assortment of obstacles and boosters, which can be struck with the ball to earn various bonuses" — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide)
- The official loop: "hit the ball as far as possible, upgrade Power, unlock Clubs, and improve your distance record" — [hit-a-golf-ball.wiki guides](https://hit-a-golf-ball.wiki/guides/)
- Mobile uses touch controls, but the fan wiki says the exact meter behaviour "must be read from the current interface" (i.e., it has not been documented) — [hit-a-golf-ball.wiki controls](https://hit-a-golf-ball.wiki/guides/controls/)
- Golf Training! loop: "build up your hitting power through training, then put it to the test by launching a ball as far as you can across multiple worlds" — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/golf-training-codes/)
- Genre convention for comparison:
  - Super Golf!: desktop "click and drag to charge… release to shoot"; mobile "adjust power with slider, tap button to shoot" — [Roblox Super Golf!](https://www.roblox.com/games/4468711919/Super-Golf)
  - Golf Frenzy!: mobile "hold and drag to adjust power, release" — [Roblox Golf Frenzy!](https://www.roblox.com/games/8755324823/Golf-Frenzy)

### Inferences
- The hook is a single satisfying "charge, release, watch it fly" moment with a numeric distance payoff, plus light steering skill during flight. The flight is a spectator moment, like "Kick a Lucky Block" or "Yeet a X" games.
- Third-party exploit scripts advertise "Auto Swing" and "Auto Rebirth" — [cheater.fun](https://cheater.fun/hacks_roblox/84584-hit-a-golf-ball-script.html); [cheatermad](https://cheatermad.com/hit-a-golf-ball-script/). This implies swing-spamming and rebirthing are the grind activities. It is not evidence of an official auto mode.

### Gaps
- No documented juice details (camera, VFX, sound, number popups) and no documented mobile button layout.

## 4. Progression: upgrades, rebirth, pets, worlds, automation

### Takeaway
Progression has four parts:
- **Power** is the core stat.
- **Clubs and balls** are bought with Coins and each carries a Power multiplier.
- **Rebirth** resets Power for a large permanent multiplier but keeps clubs and balls.
- **Worlds** are six maps (Earth, Moon, Rainbow, Candy, Mecha, Ocean), each gated by Power.

Pets from eggs are reported, but the sources partly mix in Golf Training!.

### Cited Findings
- "Your choice of club and golf balls impact how much Power you gain every time you hit the ball, with each club and golf ball having a Power multiplier attached to it. You can buy additional clubs from the in-game shop using Coins" — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide)
- Rebirth "can be performed every time you cross a specified level threshold… you gain a massive permanent Power multiplier, though it resets your Power stat… Since you keep your club and golf balls, returning to your previous Power level is not an issue" — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide)
- "Currently, there are a total of six maps: Earth, Moon, Rainbow, Candy, Mecha, and Ocean." Clearing a map transports you to the next World, and "each subsequent map unlock requires you to climb to new levels of Power" — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide)
- Pets "hatched from eggs" give "additional power multipliers" — search summary citing [hitagolfball.wiki guides](https://hitagolfball.wiki/guides/). Note that this site is the one that equates the game with Golf Training!.
- Gamepasses include "Forest Dragon, Gold Dragon, and Kraken." The other fan wiki cautions these "should not be called pets unless the current Tiny Loop interface uses that word" — [hit-a-golf-ball.wiki guides](https://hit-a-golf-ball.wiki/guides/)
- Golf Training!:
  - UPD4 "added a new zone, three eggs, twenty-plus pets, twenty-plus clubs" — [hitagolfball.wiki](https://hitagolfball.wiki/)
  - The RELEASE code gives a "Fox Pet (Legendary)", which implies a rarity tier system that includes Legendary — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/golf-training-codes/)

### Inferences
- The "keep gear through rebirth" rule softens the reset pain, which matters for a kids' audience. Dig for Dragon Eggs could keep dragons and tools through rebirth in the same way.
- Gamepass dragons (Forest Dragon, Gold Dragon) are thematically very close to our dragon pets. This is a useful price and positioning reference once prices are found.

### Gaps
- No rebirth thresholds or multiplier numbers, egg costs, pet rarity tiers or odds for the Tiny Loop game.
- No evidence of official auto-swing, auto-hatch, idle or offline earnings.
- No information on how high the numbers escalate (suffixes, etc.).

## 6. Monetization

### Takeaway
The Tiny Loop game reportedly sells about 15 gamepasses. These are twelve tiered **Power Multiplier** passes, a **x2 Money** pass, and three premium companions: **Forest Dragon, Gold Dragon and Kraken**. I found no prices or developer-product details.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
