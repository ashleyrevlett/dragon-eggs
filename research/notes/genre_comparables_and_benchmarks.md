## 1. Comparable recent hits (2025–2026) in the one-action incremental style: who, when, peak CCU, visits

### Takeaway
The 2025–2026 wave is led by games with literal, verb-first titles ("Sell Lemons", "Dig to Earth's CORE!", "Fish It!", "+1 Speed Keyboard Escape", "Be a Lucky Block +1 Speed"). They come from small or new studios and climb from a few thousand CCU to six or seven figures within weeks. The extreme tier ("+1 Speed Keyboard Escape" ~7.3M peak, Plants vs Brainrots ~8.7M, Fish It ~2.7M) shows the ceiling. Most copycats peak at 20k–200k CCU.

### Inferences
- **Title pattern:** short, literal and imperative, often with "+1 Speed" or an emoji prefix and a bracketed update tag ("[W2]", "[UPD]", "[NOW]", "[🥤]"). Update tags in titles look like a common way to signal freshness on charts.
- **Trend speed:** once a format works ("+1 Speed … Escape", "Be a …", "Dig to …"), dozens of clones appear within weeks. Timing matters more than polish for the initial spike. The durable winners (Fish It, Dig to Earth's Core) keep shipping content updates.
- **Typical outcome distribution:** most comparables peak at 20k–200k CCU and reach hundreds of millions of visits over 6–18 months. Million-plus CCU is rare.

## 2. What these games share (core loop, progression, UI, monetization)

### Takeaway
They share one repeated input (click, dig, run, fish, sell) that pays a currency, exponential upgrades (tool, speed, multiplier), zone or world gates, rebirth or prestige, and eggs/pets or collectible "brainrots" as multipliers. Monetization stacks multiplier gamepasses and convenience passes with consumable dev products and paid random pulls.

### Cited Findings
- Golf Training!/Hit a Golf Ball (listed as a structural reference, not a profile): train power, launch for distance-based coins, hatch pets from eggs, unlock stronger clubs, push into new zones. Launched 9 Mar 2026. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/hit-golf-ball-a-beginner-s-guide); [hitagolfball.wiki](https://hitagolfball.wiki/)
- Pet Simulator 99 is the genre benchmark: pet collection at scale, rebirth-style prestige, golden/rainbow variants, trading hub. — [Adellion](https://adellion.com/roblox-simulator-games)
- Dig to Earth's Core uses exponential number growth as the hook ($0.01 to $9.78M pickaxe). — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y-dQbFiUc_0)
- Sell Lemons runs ongoing events ("Orchard Event"), tier lists and codes, the standard live-ops for these games. — [selllemons.wiki](https://selllemons.wiki/)

### Inferences
- Common UI conventions in this wave (inferred from genre familiarity and the titles/guides above, not verified per game): big-number HUD, a left-side button column (Shop/Pets/Rebirth/Codes), pop-up offer prompts, and codes as a retention and social hook.

## 3. Roblox guidance: discovery signals, Rewarded Video Ads, paid random items, age/safety policy

### Takeaway
Since the 15 Jun 2026 "Optimizing Discovery" update, Recommended For You scores games over a 28-day window (D1, D2–7 and D8–28 retention). It splits qPTR into separate play-through, session-quality and spend signals, and gives heavy weight to "intentional co-play days". The update is aimed at clickbait-thumbnail and short-hook games, which is directly relevant to this genre. Rewarded video ads are open to all ad-eligible creators (since 18 Feb 2026) but can only be shown to users aged 13+. Paid random items need odds disclosure and a PolicyService eligibility check. Chat needs facial age checks globally since Jan 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Discovery**
- The Recommended For You window was expanded from 7 to 28 days, with retention signals for day 1, days 2–7 and days 8–28. — [Roblox Newsroom, Jun 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [DevForum: RFY improvements valuing long-term retention](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)
- qPTR = engaging ("qualified") plays ÷ RFY impressions. A qualified play filters out accidental clicks and quick bounces. The former qPTR signal is now split into play-through behavior, session quality and spend. — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [Creator Hub: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery); [DevForum 2025: improved RFY + analytics](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441)
- Other named signals: 7-day play days per user and 7-day intentional co-play days. Third-party coverage calls "intentional co-play days" the headline signal. — [gmmarket.me](https://gmmarket.me/community/post/how-the-roblox-discovery-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026-myths-debunked); [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/why-roblox-s-new-discovery-algorithm-favors-games-that-bring-friends-together); [Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-optimizing-discovery-how-great-games-reach-millions-of-players-on-roblox)
- Rollout began 15 Jun 2026. Roblox said the old system "could disproportionately favor games that win attention with exciting thumbnails but don't deliver long-term value". — [Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-optimizing-discovery-how-great-games-reach-millions-of-players-on-roblox); [ZehnStudio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-discovery-rollout/)
- Creator impact anecdote: Evan Baer (Turning Tables Games) said revenue fell by up to 70% in week one, then the portfolio "performing better than ever" three weeks later. — [X post by EvanZir](https://x.com/EvanZir/article/2074281929575174603); summarized by search. A follow-up DevForum post, "Boost Your Discovery by Building Games People Want to Play", exists. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042)
- Roblox analytics now lets creators view retention by acquisition source and pick a benchmark set. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157)

**Rewarded Video Ads**
- Opened to all ads-eligible creators on 18 Feb 2026 (expanded from an earlier partial rollout in 2025). Eligibility: public, unrestricted experience with more than 2,000 unique monthly visitors, no free-form user creation or AI interaction, creator 13+, ID-verified, 2FA, updated maturity questionnaire. — [DevForum: RV available to all ads-eligible creators](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rewarded-video-ads-are-now-available-to-all-ads-eligible-creators/4063278); [DevForum: More creators can use RV (2025)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-creators-can-now-use-rewarded-video-ads/3838678); [Creator Hub: Rewarded video ads](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-ads); [gmmarket.me RV 2026](https://gmmarket.me/community/post/roblox-rewarded-video-ads-in-2026-who-can-enable-them-how-to-integrate-and-what)
- Rewards for watching ads **cannot be randomized items**. Rewarded video may only be offered to users 13+ and must use Roblox's own product. — same sources; also [New Advertising Policies & Standards](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-advertising-policies-standards/4527365); [GameBiz Consulting](https://www.gamebizconsulting.com/blog/roblox-ad-monetization-guide-2026)

**Paid random items (eggs, crates, lucky blocks bought with Robux)**
- Odds must be shown for every possible item before purchase, as numeric percentages that sum to 100%. Creators must check `PolicyService` `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` and give ineligible users a compliant alternative. A 2026 DevForum post clarifies the requirements. — [Creator Hub: Paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items); [DevForum: Clarifying Requirements for Paid Random Items](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/clarifying-requirements-for-paid-random-items/4654622)

**Age and child-safety changes**
- Facial age estimation (via vendor Persona; images deleted after processing) is required to use chat. Enforcement started in the first week of Dec 2025 in Australia, New Zealand and the Netherlands, and went global in early Jan 2026. Users are sorted into brackets (<9, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18–20, 21+) and can only chat with similar ages unless they are Trusted Connections. Playing without chat still works. — [Roblox Newsroom Nov 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat); [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/gaming/news/roblox-launches-required-facial-age-checks-1236584050/); [BiometricUpdate Jan 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/roblox-rolls-out-facial-age-estimation-for-chat-access-globally); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/roblox-to-make-facial-age-checks-mandatory-to-access-any-chat-features-and-will-put-common-sense-limits-on-the-age-ranges-users-can-chat-within/)

### Inferences
- For a kids' one-action incremental game, the 28-day retention signals and co-play weighting penalize the classic "big-number spike, bored in 10 minutes" pattern. Design should target D8–28 return reasons (daily rewards, events, long rebirth tail) and in-server or friend multipliers.
- An under-13 audience cannot be monetized with rewarded video. Ads must be gated to 13+ and their rewards must be deterministic. Robux eggs need odds UI plus a non-random fallback for restricted users.
- Chat restrictions reduce social trading and coordination for young users, so co-play features that don't depend on chat (shared boosts, party bonuses) become more valuable. This is my inference.

### Gaps
- Could not read Roblox's primary pages, so the exact weights, the qualified-play threshold in minutes, and the precise spend-signal definition are unverified.
- The exact date of the paid-random-items clarification post, and which regions are restricted, were not confirmed.

## 4. Public benchmarks: D1/D7 retention, session length, payer conversion, ARPPU/ARPDAU

### Takeaway
GameAnalytics' 2026 Roblox data shows a median D1 of 10.3% and D7 of 1.6%, and a top-1% D1 of 22.2% and D7 of 9.1%. Median ARPPU is $0.70, against $4.80 for the top 5% and $55.31 for the top 1%. Platform-wide, Q2 2026 averaged $13.35 bookings per DAU for the quarter. Payer conversion "norms" (1–3% median, 2–3% for simulators) come only from low-authority secondary blogs.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox game median retention: D1 10.3%, D7 1.6%, D30 0.5%. Top 1%: D1 22.2%, D7 9.1%, D30 4.7%. — [GameDevReports summary of GameAnalytics "Key Roblox Game Metrics 2026"](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox); [GameAnalytics 2026 benchmarks](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-mobile-pc-gaming-benchmarks)
- Median ARPPU is $0.70, top 10% $2.33, top 5% $4.80, top 1% $55.31 (about 12x the top 5%). — [GameDevReports/GameAnalytics](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox)
- Genre retention claims: RPG D1 35% / D7 16% / D30 7.8%; Obby D1 18% / D7 6% / D30 1.9%. **These look like top-performer or different-sample figures and don't match the GA median. The original source is unclear, so treat as low confidence.** — [BloxG retention benchmarks by genre (2026)](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-retention-benchmarks)
- Payer conversion: about 1–3% "Roblox median", 3–5% strong ("typical of … Simulators"), above 5% top tier. Simulator/Tycoon 2–3%, Obby 1–2%. Average spend for simulators is about 200–500 Robux per payer. **Secondary blogs or glossaries with no primary data cited.** — [ZehnStudio26 glossary](https://zehn-studio26.com/glossary/conversion-rate/); [GeneralistProgrammer](https://generalistprogrammer.com/tutorials/roblox-game-monetization-complete-revenue-strategy-guide); a DevForum thread discusses poor conversion: [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/very-bad-payer-conversion-rate-for-game-monetization-how-can-i-do-better/3302200)
- Platform Q2 2026: DAU 123M (+10% YoY), hours 29B (+5%), bookings $1.6B (+8%), ABPDAU $13.35. Roblox attributed the bookings shortfall to lower per-hour monetization, "most notably with younger cohorts in the U.S. and Canada". — [Roblox Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Roblox-Q2-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf); [SEC 8-K ex99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm); [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q2-2026-slides-bookings-growth-slows-to-8-amid-user-decline-93CH-4826401)

### Inferences
- Platform ABPDAU of $13.35 is a **quarterly** figure, not daily ARPDAU. Divided by about 91 days it implies roughly $0.15 per DAU per day platform-wide (my arithmetic). An individual experience earns far less, because Roblox keeps most of the bookings and much spend goes to avatar items.
- A reasonable target for a new one-action incremental game is D1 at or above 15–20% and D7 at or above 5%, which is near the GA top-1% band and enough to survive the 28-day RFY signals. This is an inference, not a published target.
- 29B hours ÷ 123M DAU over about 91 days is roughly 2.6 hours per DAU per day **across all experiences** (my arithmetic, not a session-length benchmark).

### Gaps
- No reliable public per-genre average session length or first-session pacing (minutes to first rebirth or first egg) was found. This needs first-party analytics or teardown work.
- No public ARPDAU per experience. No primary-source payer conversion benchmark from Roblox or GameAnalytics was retrievable.
- Monthly unique payers and the age-cohort split from the Q2 2026 letter could not be read (sec.gov was blocked).

## 5. Typical Robux price points (gamepasses and dev products) in the genre

### Takeaway
Simulator gamepasses cluster at about 50–800 Robux. 2x Coins runs about 149–799, Auto-clicker about 50–399, VIP about 349. No verified per-game price tables for the 2025–2026 wave were retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- 2x Coins: 365 R$ (Washiez Car Wash), 149 R$ (another game), 799 R$ (Saber Simulator). — [Rolimons 2X Coins](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/18225070); [Rolimons x2 Coins](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/1821800280); [Saber Simulator wiki](https://roblox-saber-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses)
- Auto-clicker: 50 R$ (Mega Noob Simulator), 399 R$ (Bonking Simulator), 129 R$ ("Auto Clicks!", Clicker League). — [Rolimons Autoclicker](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/8809672); [Rolimons Auto Clicker](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/14777766); [Rolimons Auto Clicks!](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/55506701)
- VIP: 349 R$ (Saber Simulator). — [Saber Simulator wiki](https://roblox-saber-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses)
- Other common pass types in clicker games: "2x Clicks" and "2x Offline Clicker". — [Rolimons 2x Clicks](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/664795557); [Rolimons 2x Offline](https://www.rolimons.com/gamepass/98004837)

### Inferences
- A sensible ladder for a kids' one-action game (inferred): a cheap entry pass at 25–99 R$ (e.g. +pet slot or auto-click), core multipliers at 149–399 R$ (2x money, 2x speed, auto-farm), and VIP or "best value" at 399–799 R$. Dev products would be consumable boosts or currency packs at about 10–100 R$ and Robux eggs or lucky blocks at about 50–500 R$, with odds UI required.

### Gaps
- Most price examples are from older simulators (Saber, Bonking, Clicker League), not the 2025–2026 hits. Current passes for Sell Lemons, Dig to Earth's Core, Fish It and +1 Speed Keyboard Escape should be pulled straight from their Roblox store tabs.
- No public dev-product price distribution data was found.
